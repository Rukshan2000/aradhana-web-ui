# What We Need From You

Every section below is a piece of the invitation page you will get. If you skip one,
that part of the page is simply left out or we fill it with something tasteful.

---

## 1. The Two of You
*(this becomes the top of the page — the first thing a guest sees)*

- Bride's name, the way you want it shown (a short name works best — "Tharu", not the full name on the ID)
- Groom's name, the same way
- Your city or town — "Kandy, Sri Lanka"
- A hashtag or short line, if you have one — "#TharuWedsSithika" (optional)
- A one-line welcome, if you want your own wording instead of ours
  (ours: "Together with their families")

## 2. The Day
*(the date, the countdown, and the "when and where" cards)*

- Date of the wedding, and the start time
- How you want the date written out on the page — "Saturday, the twelfth of December" —
  or just leave it to us
- **For each event on the day (or nearby days) — poruwa, church, homecoming, reception, after-party:**
  - What to call it — "The Ceremony", "The Reception"
  - Time, in words if you like — "4:30 in the afternoon"
  - Venue name — "St. Anne's Chapel"
  - Address
  - One small note for guests, if any — "Please be seated by 4:15"
- **Order of the evening** (optional, but guests love it) — a simple time-by-time list:
  - 4:30 pm — Ceremony
  - 6:30 pm — Cocktails on the terrace
  - 8:30 pm — Toasts and first dance

## 3. Your Story
*(a scrolling section, one moment at a time — usually 3, up to 5)*

For each moment:
- The year — "2019"
- A short title — "The first evening"
- Two or three lines about it
- One photo for that moment (optional)

If writing is not your thing, just tell us the story on a call. We write it and you approve it.

## 4. Photos

- **One hero photo** — the big one at the top of the page. Landscape works best.
- **10 to 30 photos of the two of you** for the gallery
- **A photo of the venue**, if you have one
- Photos for the story moments above, if you want them
- Send them straight from your phone gallery. No need to rename or resize anything.

Tell us if any photo must not go on the page.

## 5. How It Should Look

- A song to play softly when the page opens (optional) — name it, or send the file

---

## The Short Version

If you only send us three things today, send these:

1. Both names
2. The date, time and place
3. One good photo of the two of you

We can build the page from that and fill in the rest as it comes.
